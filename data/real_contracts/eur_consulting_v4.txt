ARTIKEL 5: VERGUTUNG UND ZAHLUNGSBEDINGUNGEN (Fees and Payment Terms)

The not-to-exceed amount of EUR 62,000 per month covers all consulting
hours, travel, and ancillary expenses under this agreement. Vendor shall
not invoice amounts exceeding this monthly ceiling without written
authorization via change request.

5.2 Payment Terms
Invoices are payable within 30 days. Late payment interest applies at a
rate of 3% per month on overdue amounts, consistent with applicable EU
commercial law.
