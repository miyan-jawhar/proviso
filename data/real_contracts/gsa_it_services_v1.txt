ARTICLE 7 — PAYMENT TERMS AND BILLING LIMITATIONS

7.1 Monthly Service Fee Cap
The maximum monthly billing cap of USD 45,000 applies to all professional
services rendered under this agreement. Invoices exceeding this amount in any
calendar month shall be subject to dispute resolution under Article 12.

7.2 Late Payment Penalty
In the event of payment delay exceeding 30 days from invoice date, a late
payment fee of 1.5% per month shall be applied to the outstanding balance.

7.3 Data Protection
Vendor agrees to maintain compliance with applicable federal data protection
requirements under FISMA and applicable state privacy regulations. A Data
Processing Agreement addendum is incorporated herein as Exhibit B.

ARTICLE 11 — TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with 90 days written notice.
Early termination by Client shall incur a fee not to exceed $12,000.
