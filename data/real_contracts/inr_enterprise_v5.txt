CLAUSE 7: PAYMENT AND BILLING

7.1 Monthly Cap
The maximum monthly billing cap of INR 18,00,000 (Eighteen Lakh) applies
to all professional IT services rendered under this Agreement, excluding GST.

7.2 Late Fees
In case of payment delay beyond 45 days, the Service Provider may levy a
late fee of 1.8% per month on the outstanding amount.

CLAUSE 11: DATA PROTECTION
Both parties agree to comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the
IT (Amendment) Act 2008, and applicable Personal Data Protection guidelines.
No personal data processing agreement is currently in place pending
finalization of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 framework.

CLAUSE 15: TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 60 days written
notice. Premature termination by Client shall attract a penalty not exceeding
INR 5,00,000.
